# Opportunities and creative brief

Read `knowledge/learnings.md`, intake, research summary, evidence log and signals before proposing work.

First run `prepare <client> <project> strategy`. Read `.agents/skills/axis-opportunity-engine/SKILL.md`. Create a small field of distinct opportunities. Ten is a maximum, not a target. Each route needs evidence, counter-evidence, fit, originality, feasibility and a clear answer to why this artist or brand should make it now. Do not manufacture urgency.

Present the opportunities with tradeoffs and a recommendation, then stop. The user selects with `APPROVE OPPORTUNITY 03`, or another numbered route. A recommendation is not approval.

After current opportunity approval, run `prepare <client> <project> brief`. Use `skills/axis-positioning/SKILL.md` when the market frame needs clarification, then `.agents/skills/axis-creative-strategy/SKILL.md`. The brief must name the approved opportunity, identity or music truth, audience tension, proposition, emotional target, required outputs and evidence limits.

Set completed files to `READY_FOR_REVIEW`. Do not move into concepts until the chosen opportunity remains approved and the brief is complete.
