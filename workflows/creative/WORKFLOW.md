# Five concepts and concept approval

Run status and require current opportunity approval. Read the chosen opportunity, creative brief, `skills/ci-creative-director/SKILL.md` and the relevant artist-release or brand-campaign module. Run `prepare <client> <project> concepts`.

Read the director history returned by preparation, plus `knowledge/learnings.md`. Follow [director feedback guidance](../../references/director-taste.md). Use relevant reasons to avoid repeating corrections. Preserve room for contrasting ideas, and explain any conflict with current project needs. Do not turn one project's rejection into a ban across clients.

Develop five genuinely different creative mechanisms around the selected opportunity. They are complete territories, not five names or five visual styles for one idea. Every concept must explain how the artist, music, release, brand or product is essential to the idea.

Complete concept 01 through 05 and the comparison. Score the ten criteria with reasons. Scores are judgments, not measured results. Check originality against the inspected reference set without claiming exhaustive clearance. Apply `skills/ci-performance-creative/SKILL.md` after ideation to test comprehension, hook, proof and action without flattening the idea.

Present all five with strengths, weaknesses, risks and a recommendation, then stop. Only the user's explicit choice records concept approval. Refining a concept does not approve it.

Capture any supplied selection and rejection reasons with the approval, in the user's words. Reasons are optional. Never invent missing feedback.
