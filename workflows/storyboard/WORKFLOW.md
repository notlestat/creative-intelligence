Require current art-direction approval. Run `prepare <client> <project> storyboard`. Read `skills/axis-visual-video/SKILL.md` and `.agents/skills/axis-storyboard/SKILL.md`, particularly dramaturgy and universal direction, with provider syntax excluded.

Create treatment.md, storyboard.md, shot-list.md and sound-plan.md. Fill shot-plan.json as the machine-checked shot source. Ensure the written cards and JSON agree on shot ID, order, duration, composition, movement, product detail, sound and continuity. No shot exists only to look attractive.

Write every storyboard field in the template, including time, purpose, exact visual, subject, composition, camera, lens feel, light, action, product, text, audio, transition, continuity and Melius notes. Each shot-plan entry also needs start/end frames, constraints, references, planned variants, attempts per variant, risk reason, validation check and fallback. Use NONE with explanation for inapplicable direction. Actual Melius costs and support remain unknown until documented.

For a 15s paid-social ad, consider 0-2 hook, 2-5 tension, 5-10 development, 10-13 payoff and 13-15 brand/CTA. Adapt to the idea and comprehension; do not force every narrative into that timing. Check first-second attention, sound-off understanding, eye trace, object/identity continuity and total duration.

Present the complete storyboard and shot list. Record `APPROVE STORYBOARD` and `APPROVE SHOT LIST` separately from authentic user decisions. Do not infer one from the other. Any shot-plan edit stales both approvals and needs review.
