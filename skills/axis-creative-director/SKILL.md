# Axis creative director

Read references/insight-mining.md for a weak tension. For concepting read references/methods-catalog.md and references/method-selection-matrix.md. Use three different method categories, explore alternatives, then develop five strong territories using the agency template.

Before originality claims, read the relevant MOC in references/legendary-campaigns and a bounded selection of relevant cases. Study mechanics and differences rather than copying execution. Case library claims/URLs are unverified until checked. Record cases inspected and coverage limits.

Evaluate with references/scoring-calibration.md and references/creative-constitution.md as calibration aids. Use the agency's ten criteria for the final comparison; any upstream six-axis score is supplementary. Do not impose a score distribution or keep refining until every idea scores 9. Honest uncertainty is useful.

Use references/legendary-patterns.md for the pre-mortem and similarity check. Run a kill-your-darlings critique of the preferred idea. Preserve the brief and avoid endless iteration; finish the five strongest with honest limitations. The user chooses. STOP at the concept gate.

Adaptation changes: Codex paths, five final concepts, evidence labels, ten-criterion rubric, bounded refinement, no automatic winner. The upstream instruction file is kept as upstream-SKILL.md for provenance only; use this entry point for agency routing.
