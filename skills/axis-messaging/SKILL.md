# Axis messaging

Map product detail to benefit, customer relevance and source evidence. Read references/messaging-example-mia.md only for useful structural examples. Use the agency copy template. Keep the selected concept's mechanism, emotional register and single-minded proposition intact.

No superlatives, proof quotas, imaginary testimonials or forced long descriptions. The brand voice is supplied by this client. UNKNOWN proof stays unknown and cannot become published copy. Offer only the variants needed by the brief.
