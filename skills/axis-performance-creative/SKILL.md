# Axis performance creative

Use the approved creative brief and client tone. Read references/ad-playbook.md for the relevant hook or iteration issue; references/marketing-principles.md only where a principle explains the choice. Use references/video-script-templates.md or references/image-creative-templates.md as optional structural prompts, not quotas.

First review whether the audience can understand the action, proposition, product role and appropriate next step. Offer a precise correction that preserves the distinctive mechanism. For copy, write on-screen lines, VO, headline, primary text, CTA and useful alternative hooks. Give each variation a test hypothesis.

This adaptation removes the upstream aggressive-tone default, mandatory type-selection detour, 5x5x5 quotas, imported market assumptions, generation tools and Remotion production. Do not invent urgency, testimonials, numeric results or proof to fill a framework. Do not export to an unrelated vault or ad platform.

Platform references may be stale or mix recommended lengths with hard caps. Verify current official documentation for the selected placement and date it. Metrics require supplied data and limitations. Use NOT_ASSESSED/UNKNOWN where evidence is absent.

Original retained as upstream-SKILL.md; the agency workflow is the operating contract.
