# Axis visual video

Read references/dramaturgy.md then references/universal-rules.md for the universal filmmaking methods. Read references/role-modes.md for treatment/storyboard tasks, references/camera-lighting-vocabulary.md for precise direction and references/fixes-and-skeletons.md for continuity problems. Only load relevant sections.

For each shot specify its communication purpose, action, geography, eye trace, motivated movement, light, physical details, timing, sound and start/end state. Use emotion through observable action. Check transitions and continuity across the sequence.

Apply methodology with judgment to product films and non-character concepts. Do not add a person, obstacle, dramatic pause or fixed beat pattern merely to satisfy a narrative template. The approved idea controls the structure. Every shot must still have a reason to exist.

Ignore provider-specific syntax/examples in these references for Melius. Do not choose Seedance/Kling/Veo or copy their flags, clip limits or reference tokens. Use skills/melius-production at the agency root for universal briefs. No generation calls.

Adaptation changes: namespaced entry, human approvals, Melius-only production handoff, universal syntax and no forced drama. Original retained as upstream-SKILL.md.
