# Axis audience

Choose only the matching B2B ICP, buyer, user or B2C reference template under references/. Separate purchase role, situation, problem, desire, objection, current alternative and awareness. Use sourced observed language and sampling limits.

Templates illustrate fields, not evidence. Never generate a realistic quote and present it as customer language. Do not invent age, income, personality or behaviour to fill blank fields. Use UNKNOWN or HYPOTHESIS with validation plan. Actual customer research must be collected independently.

Adaptation changes: removes minimum bullet quotas and synthetic realistic quotes, uses agency evidence labels and research output location. Original retained as upstream-SKILL.md.
