# Axis positioning

Use sourced product capabilities, audience evidence, current alternatives and client learnings. Work from who cares, what problem matters, available alternatives, distinctive value and evidence. references/positioning-example-mia.md illustrates structure only, never facts about this client.

Write a short positioning statement and evidence-linked rationale in the agency brief. Do not require seven bullets, invent competitor weaknesses or fill geography tiers without evidence. Mark proposed positioning and unsupported insight as hypotheses. Campaign ideation belongs to the creative director.

Adaptation changes: evidence controls, no fixed volume quotas, agency paths and no invented comparisons. Original retained as upstream-SKILL.md.
