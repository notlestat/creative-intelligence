# Axis audience research
## Purpose
Describe who cares, in what situation and why, without decorative personas.
## Inputs
Interviews, reviews, comments, support material, sales notes, product behaviour and explicit client hypotheses.
## Process
1. Separate buyer, user and cultural participant where relevant.
2. Extract situations, jobs, alternatives, desires, objections and exact language.
3. Mark client beliefs as supplied claims.
4. Turn gaps into validation questions.
## Evidence requirements
Quotes remain short, attributed and linked. Demographics appear only when supplied or sourced and relevant.
## Outputs
Audience evidence cards and confidence-scored hypotheses.
## Failure modes
Invented names or quotes, unsupported age and income bands, averaging distinct audiences into one persona.
## Quality checklist
- Situations are more specific than traits.
- Purchase role is clear.
- Exact language is traceable.
- No blank is filled for completeness.
