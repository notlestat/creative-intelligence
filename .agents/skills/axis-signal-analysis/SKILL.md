# Axis signal analysis
## Purpose
Name patterns that matter creatively without presenting weak evidence as a trend.
## Inputs
Classified findings, source dates, sample notes and counter-evidence.
## Process
1. Group findings by audience, culture, visual, competitor, product, narrative, behaviour or community.
2. State the repeatable pattern in plain language.
3. Score strength, confidence and freshness separately.
4. Write the creative implication and the strongest counter-evidence.
## Evidence requirements
A signal has at least one explicit evidence link. STRONG requires repeated independent evidence, not repeated copies.
## Outputs
Signal title, description, evidence, strength, confidence, freshness, implication and counter-evidence.
## Failure modes
Trend language from one source, circular evidence, confusing relevance with strength, hiding stale data.
## Quality checklist
- Strength and confidence can differ.
- Dates are visible.
- The implication does not overclaim.
- PERFORMANCE remains reserved until real data exists.
