# Axis opportunity engine
## Purpose
Turn research and signals into a small field of meaningful creative possibilities.
## Inputs
Project memory, approved research set, signals, constraints and current objective.
## Process
1. Write possibilities across campaign, content, product story, visual, collaboration, editorial and physical work as relevant.
2. Remove duplicates and routes that merely restyle the same idea.
3. Connect each route to project truth and evidence.
4. Score originality, fit, evidence and feasibility.
5. Mark at most one recommendation, never a winner.
## Evidence requirements
Each opportunity has evidence and counter-evidence. `why now` cannot rely on invented urgency.
## Outputs
Up to ten full opportunity records ready for approve, reject, save, edit or why review.
## Failure modes
Ad-only thinking, generic trends, ideas detached from product, unsupported certainty, volume over distinction.
## Quality checklist
- Every route answers the core question differently.
- Risks are real.
- Scores have written reasons.
- Human selection is still required.
