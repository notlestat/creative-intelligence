# Axis creative handoff
## Purpose
Give each collaborator only the decisions, references and constraints needed to do the work.
## Inputs
Approved strategy, art direction, storyboard or shot list, named recipient, assets, formats, timeline and rights.
## Process
1. Name the recipient and their decisions.
2. Remove internal reasoning that does not help production.
3. Include concept, emotional target, relevant specifications, references, do and do-not rules.
4. Add unknowns and required confirmations.
5. Produce a document and optional Excalidraw board.
## Evidence requirements
Only approved material enters the handoff. Asset links and reference rights remain visible.
## Outputs
Recipient-specific handoff, exportable board and sign-off state.
## Failure modes
One generic brief for everyone, leaking internal chain-of-thought, stale approvals, missing formats or ownership.
## Quality checklist
- Recipient can act without a second strategy meeting.
- No irrelevant section remains.
- Unknowns have owners.
- Final human approval is recorded.
