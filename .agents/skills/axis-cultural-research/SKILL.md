# Axis cultural research
## Purpose
Find relevant shifts, tensions and conversation patterns that may change what the project can credibly make.
## Inputs
Project question, audience evidence, locations, date range, keywords and available source providers.
## Process
1. Declare scope and provider availability.
2. Gather dated material across more than one source type where possible.
3. Group recurring language or behaviours.
4. Record counterexamples and sampling limits.
5. Promote a pattern to SIGNAL only when evidence repeats.
## Evidence requirements
Every observation links to captured material and date. Weak samples stay WEAK or EMERGING.
## Outputs
Culture findings, possible signals and unanswered research questions.
## Failure modes
Calling one post a trend, reading sentiment into sarcasm, hiding inaccessible platforms, recency without relevance.
## Quality checklist
- Scope and sample are visible.
- Repetition is counted.
- Counter-evidence is included.
- No cultural claim depends on model intuition alone.
