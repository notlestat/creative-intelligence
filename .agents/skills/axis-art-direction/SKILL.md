# Axis art direction
## Purpose
Specify how the idea looks and moves, with every visual decision tied to the concept.
## Inputs
Approved creative direction, visual identity, references, formats, budget, timeline and rights constraints.
## Process
1. Define visual thesis and emotional target.
2. Specify colour, contrast, texture, material, light, composition, camera, people, styling, location, type and motion.
3. Separate inspiration references from execution references.
4. Write precise do and do-not rules.
## Evidence requirements
References are sourced and labelled. Product claims and locations are verified before production.
## Outputs
Art-direction record ready for review and later handoff.
## Failure modes
"More cinematic", competitor copying, moodboard justified after sourcing, impossible production direction.
## Quality checklist
- Each decision serves the idea.
- Full distribution variants are considered.
- Rights and licensing questions are present.
- Human art-direction approval remains explicit.
