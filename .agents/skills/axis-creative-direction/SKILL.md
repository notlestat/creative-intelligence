# Axis creative direction
## Purpose
Translate an approved strategy into a project-wide aesthetic and narrative standard.
## Inputs
Approved creative strategy, identity memory, audience context, distribution and production constraints.
## Process
1. Name the world in one exact line.
2. Define story, tone, role of product or artist and emotional movement.
3. Set principles that govern image, copy, motion, sound and physical work.
4. State what the direction refuses.
## Evidence requirements
The direction cites the approved insight and identity evidence it extends.
## Outputs
Creative world statement, principles, channel behaviour, boundaries and evaluation questions.
## Failure modes
Mood words without decisions, generic cinema language, identity reinvention without approval, unrelated execution ideas.
## Quality checklist
- A collaborator can judge new work against it.
- Principles cause visible choices.
- Refusals protect distinction.
- It remains broad enough for multiple executions.
