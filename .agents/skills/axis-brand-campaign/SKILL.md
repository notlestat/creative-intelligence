# Axis brand campaign
## Purpose
Turn a product or brand truth into work that can live across selected touchpoints.
## Inputs
Brand memory, approved opportunity, product proof, launch context, channels, budget and production constraints.
## Process
1. Choose the campaign role: launch, product story, editorial, social, film, photography, collaboration or activation.
2. Define the hero execution.
3. Add supporting work only when it advances the same idea.
4. Plan variants before production.
5. Check every claim against evidence.
## Evidence requirements
Product, price, performance and sustainability claims need current client-approved proof.
## Outputs
Campaign system, channel roles, variants, production questions and risks.
## Failure modes
Assuming paid ads, generic channel sprawl, unsupported claims, product added after the idea.
## Quality checklist
- Product is integral.
- One idea survives every format.
- Variant needs are concrete.
- Approval does not authorise media spend or generation.
