# Axis brand intelligence
## Purpose
Create a durable account of what the brand is, says, shows and can credibly claim.
## Inputs
Client brief, website, product material, existing identity, interviews and dated public sources.
## Process
1. Inventory identity, positioning, products, audience, voice, visual language, history and constraints.
2. Split each statement into one claim.
3. Attach confidence and evidence, or write UNKNOWN.
4. Record conflicts without resolving them by guesswork.
## Evidence requirements
Facts need a source. Client statements stay attributed. Interpretation is labelled INFERENCE.
## Outputs
Structured brand memory entries with `value`, `confidence` and `evidence[]`.
## Failure modes
Polishing client language into fact, filling blank fields, confusing current identity with desired identity.
## Quality checklist
- Every claim is traceable.
- Unknowns remain visible.
- Constraints include legal and production limits.
- A later strategist can tell what changed and why.
