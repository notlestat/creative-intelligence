# Axis creative strategy
## Purpose
Define the idea before execution choices begin.
## Inputs
Approved opportunity, evidence, brand or artist memory, objective and constraints.
## Process
1. Restate the insight and creative problem.
2. Define tension, proposition, big idea, narrative and line.
3. Set the emotional target and non-negotiable creative principles.
4. Describe one hero and only the supporting executions the idea earns.
## Evidence requirements
Strategy may interpret evidence but cannot create new facts, claims or testimonials.
## Outputs
Strategic insight, problem, tension, proposition, big idea, narrative, message, emotional target, principles, executions and risks.
## Failure modes
Execution before idea, slogan without tension, product missing from the idea, channel list mistaken for strategy.
## Quality checklist
- The big idea solves the stated problem.
- One sentence explains the project role.
- Supporting executions share one idea.
- Material changes return to human review.
