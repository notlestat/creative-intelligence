# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-09-09

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** An internal creative-intelligence workspace that helps Axis decide what a brand or artist should create next.

**What it does:** Axis stores source material, builds evidence-linked memory, identifies signals and opportunities, and develops human-selected opportunities into visual direction and handoffs. The founder remains Creative Director.

**Product category:** Internal creative research and direction tool

**Product type:** Private internal web application

**Business model:** Internal capability for Axis client work. No SaaS pricing or public accounts in v0.1.

## Target Audience

**Primary user:** The founder and creative director of Axis.

**Primary use case:** Decide what a real client brand or artist should create next, with visible evidence and an editable visual direction.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Organise project truth without inventing missing facts.
- Turn research patterns into distinct creative opportunities.
- Give collaborators a brief they can act on.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Research, references, ideas and decisions get separated across tools, making provenance and creative reasoning hard to recover.

**Why alternatives fall short:** Generic assistants blur evidence with proposal. Moodboards often show taste without the argument. Long reports do not give producers the exact direction they need.

## Differentiation

- Evidence classifications stay visible throughout the workflow.
- Human taste has an explicit decision record.
- Brand and artist projects share a system but keep specialised memory and outputs.
- Excalidraw turns the final reasoning into an editable working board.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Calm, exact and creatively opinionated.

**Style:** Plain English, short labels, no hype, no AI theatre.

## Proof Points

No real-client outcomes exist yet. Demo evidence is fictional and labelled.

## Goals

**Business goal:** Use v0.1 on the first real Axis client and learn which parts of the decision workflow save time or improve the work.

**Conversion action:** Create or reopen a project and make a traceable creative decision.
